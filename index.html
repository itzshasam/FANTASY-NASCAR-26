Commisioner • Posted 6/26/26
The points your selected driver earns during the race — including stage points and final finishing position points — will be added to your season total.
Season Points Format
Commisioner • Posted 6/26/26
Points accumulate across the entire season with a reset after race 26 when the chase starts.

Bonus points are awarded and added to your total to start
the chase based on your regular season finishing position.

The competitor with the highest total points at the end of the final race of the year is the season champion.
