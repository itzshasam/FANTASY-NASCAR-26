Proposals

Proposal 1 - Driver Selection Limits

During the regular season, each participant may select the same driver a maximum of 2 times.
When the playoffs begin, driver usage resets.
During the playoffs, each participant may select the same driver a maximum of 2 times.
This rule encourages strategy and prevents over-reliance on dominant drivers.

Passed (6-0)

Proposal 2 - Buy-in increase

The entry fee increases from $10 to $20 per participant.
The additional funds will increase the prize pool, making the league more competitive and rewarding.

Passed (7-0)

Proposal 3 - Last Place Punishment

The participant who finishes last in the season standings is required to complete a punishment chosen by the league.
Punishment must be agreed upon before the season begins (e.g., social media challenge, photo punishment, buying the winner a gift, etc.).
Ensures engagement and adds entertainment value throughout the season.

Passed (6-0)

Proposal 4 - SVG Restriction

SVG can't be chosen at road course/streetcourse races.
This rule allows for a more diverse selection at road courses given SVG's dominance at road courses.

Failed (1-6)
