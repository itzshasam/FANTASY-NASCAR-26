ABOUT

NFOCUS Fantasy NASCAR was created by Sam Cook and Dakota Ramseier with one goal in mind — to bring the excitement and strategy of fantasy football into the world of NASCAR.

We’re passionate NASCAR fans who watch every race, and we wanted a way to make each lap, pit stop, and position change mean even more.

With Fantasy NASCAR, we are not just cheering for our favorite drivers anymore —
we are competing, strategizing, and staying locked in from the green flag to the checkered flag.

Every race matters. Every pick matters. Welcome to NFOCUS — where the race is always on.
