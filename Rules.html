2026 Rule Set

Subject to Change Each Season

Rule 1:

Each participant must select one driver for every race. All driver selections must be submitted before the green flag waves. Once the race begins, no changes can be made.

Rule 2:
The points your selected driver earns during the race — including stage points and final finishing position points — will be added to your season total.

Rule 3:
Points accumulate across the entire season with a reset after race 26 when the chase starts.

Bonus points are awarded and added to your total to start
the chase based on your regular season finishing position.

The competitor with the highest total points at the end of the final race of the year is the season champion.

Rule 4:
Buy in for the league will be $20 before season starts.

Rule 5:
During the regular season, each participant may select the same driver a maximum of 2 times.
When the playoffs begin, driver usage resets.

Rule 6:
The participant who finishes last in the season standings is required to complete a punishment chosen by the league.
